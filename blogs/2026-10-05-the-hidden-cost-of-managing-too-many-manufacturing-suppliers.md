Manufacturers today work with more suppliers than ever before, but a larger supplier base does not always create more value. This article explores the hidden costs of supplier complexity, including procurement overhead, MRO purchasing inefficiencies, and reduced supply chain visibility. Learn how manufacturers can balance supplier diversification and consolidation, reduce procurement costs, improve operational efficiency, and build a more resilient supplier network.

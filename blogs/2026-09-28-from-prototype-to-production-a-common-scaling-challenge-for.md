Singapore is a leading advanced manufacturing hub, with manufacturing contributing 21.6% of GDP. However, moving from prototype to mass production can create challenges in production readiness, supplier coordination, quality, costs and delivery. As Southeast Asia’s manufacturing ecosystem grows, Singapore companies can access regional production capabilities while improving supply chain flexibility.
